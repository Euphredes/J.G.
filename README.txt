           Introduction 
           ------------ 

           Culture 

To cultivate, to preserve. Those two 
objectives make language seem to be a 
minor thing compared to motions. A 
cult must be self-cultivating and 
self-preserving because it has the 
building blocks of a culture. 

           Master 
           ------ 

           Chevalier 

I am a Master, from a cult. Culture, 
cultivating. These are places a cult 
can motion for and to. A tryhard I 
am. That earned me the rare rank: 
Chevalier. 

There is one public ritual: expose 
the virtues. Many times I've done 
this. Never have I seen a fellow 
cultmember do it. 

There is one violent ritual, very 
private. I took on the most dangerous 
acts. That earned the cult many 
demonstrations. 

There is a conclave. I earned: the 
Hunter Award. 

There is an encampment. I caused: 
teachings, against the cult, to find 
more discipline. 

To be a Master, one must rise through 
rotating ranks. The singular act of 
the Master is to remind the cult of 
the virtues as we work them. 

           Virtues 
           ------- 

           Candles 

First, love family and friends. Second, 
praise and respect rarity. Third, be 
nice. Fourth, have a partner or more to 
build or destory with. Fifth, be 
literal. Sixth, be a caregiver. Seventh, 
guard the culture that one is in, with 
noncultmembers. 

           Initiation 
           ---------- 

           Repeat 

A noncultmember must repeat words from 
the Master and show the Master what the 
Master requires to become a cultmember 
with the Master. 

           Fact 

Repetition forms patterns. 
Patterns arrive more than once. 
Frequency forms: evident. 
A fact has been established. 

           Her 

I will not be initiating anyone. Space 
must be established for the witch. 
That is my cult activity. 

           Geometry 
           -------- 

           Point 

A point: a dot: a period. That is where 
a person or thing is. 

           Line 

A line: a point reaches another point: 
motion: travel: space is established: a 
place. A line changes the where of a 
person or thing. 

           Plane 

Multiple points and lines: many moving 
parts. 

In geometry, a person learns to respect 
each point and each line. Only then 
could a place of people and things 
become manageable. That is a plane: a 
block: a layout: clean connects of 
points and lines, of people and things 
in the place.
